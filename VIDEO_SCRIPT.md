## 💡 Pro Tips for Recording

1. **Test audio first** - Do a quick test recording
2. **Clear screen** - Close unnecessary windows/tabs
3. **Slow down** - Type slower than normal (viewers follow easier)
4. **Pause** - 1-2 second pauses between sections
5. **Zoom in** - Terminal text should be large
6. **Use dark theme** - Easier on eyes
7. **Record twice** - Do two takes, pick the better one
8. **Save project** - Keep project file for edits
